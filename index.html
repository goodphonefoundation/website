Who We Are

Big Tech didn’t just give us tools — they built entire ecosystems around our lives. From the moment we pick up our phones, we’re nudged, tracked, and shaped in ways that serve their goals, not ours.

We believe that we, the people, deserve better.

That’s why we’re here: to help you see through the manipulative systems baked into modern technology, take back control, and build a digital life that feels like it actually belongs to you.

We’re not against technology. We love what good tech can do. But we are against letting it slide into our lives without question. Tools should support us — not trap us. Not demand our attention. Not quietly reshape our habits and relationships.

Everything we build and share is guided by our core value system: truth-telling, real autonomy, resilience that lasts, and a healthier relationship with the digital world. Together, we’re taking back control and building a tech life that finally feels like it belongs to you - not to them.
