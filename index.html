ABOUT

Why we exist

Big Tech built powerful tools — and powerful habits. We help people see those systems clearly, step outside them, and rebuild a tech life that feels intentional, sane, and human.

MISSION

Help people outsmart the system

Our mission is to give people the knowledge and skills to outgrow Big Tech dependency — without panic, drama, or tech snobbery.

PHILOSOPHY

Not anti-tech. Anti autopilot.

We're not here to tell you to throw your phone away. We're here to help you stop living on autopilot inside someone else's business model.

VALUES

Truth. Autonomy. Balance.

  • Truth-telling about how platforms work
  • Autonomy through real alternatives and skills
  • Balance so tech supports, not replaces, real life

PROGRAMS

What we do

From blunt guides to family programs, everything we build is designed to get you out of default mode — and into intentional, independent digital choices.

FLAGSHIP · GUIDE

thecloud.sucks

Our main project. A direct, practical guide that shows you how to step away from Big Tech's cloud services — without blowing up your life.

  • Map your current Big Tech dependencies
  • Compare privacy-friendly alternatives
  • Migrate email, storage, photos & calendars step by step
  • Keep your digital world functional while you switch
